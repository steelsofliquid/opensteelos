# OpenSteel/OS Deveopers' Code of Conduct
*Currently, and until I finish the tutorial, the only coder will be me, but that doesn't mean you can't help out in other ways.

## Expectations
Don't expect to be getting involved in a major project. I don't know if this go anywhere, so you shouldn't expect this project to be successful. Furthermore, it may demoralize you through the amount of work you presume you'll have to do if you expect to be working on a major project.
 - *I made this mistake, and so I'm limiting my goals to a select number. Check the roadmap for more.*
 - *Most projects tend to end in obscurity, even if rediscovered later. The ones you know well succeeded.*

## Treatment
A core ideal of mine is to treat everyone equally with respect. You should do the same, even if you've been jabbed at (so have I).
 - *Don't harass or attack anyone curious in the project or working on it.*
 - *Don't troll or contribute to trolls' activities.*
 - *Don't hog an aspect all to yourself. This project was started from a tutorial, mind you!*
 - *Don't ask for social or private favours among developers.*

## Honesty and Clarity
Be honest! We all may copy our fair share of code from somewhere on the internet, and we all make mistakes.
 - *If you copied a piece of code from somewhere, give credit to where you found it.*
 - *Don't copy code from proprietary works or copyrighted material not under a open source license.*
 - *Check for licensing to ensure you don't cause any licensing issues.*

## Be proud of your contributions
Even if OpenSteel/OS doesn't go far, at least be proud of the effort you gave.
 - *Please accept providing your name somewhere in a final build.*
